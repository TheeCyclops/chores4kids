# v0.1.14 - Chore library and routines foundation

Adds the opt-in workflow backend and bundled Home Assistant card for:

- Reusable chore library, opening windows, due times, and calendar/completion-based
  recurrence, including monthly and quarterly intervals.
- One outstanding occurrence per chore and overdue takeovers with parent-approved
  credit to the actual completer.
- Manually ordered personal routines, immediate points per checked step, and an
  extra parent-approved bonus for finishing all steps on time.
- Protected activation, private complete-store backup and balance snapshot,
  read-only legacy chore history, and audited accounting/retry protection.

## Compatibility and installation

Updating installs the capability; it does not activate the replacement workflow.
Existing chores, balances, rewards, purchases and settings remain in their current
workflow until explicit activation. No live migration or balance capture was
performed while preparing this release.

Before activation, complete the MagicMirror workflow-v2 integration, retain and
verify a current recovery backup and each person's exact balance, then follow
[the activation and API guide](../WORKFLOW_V2.md). Do not downgrade activated
workflow storage to old code without a compatible tested restore plan.

Install through HACS from `TheeCyclops/chores4kids`. The release asset is
`chores4kids.zip`, with integration files at its root and the matching card in
`www/chores4kids-card.js`. Home Assistant installation/restart and household
acceptance remain separate from publication.
